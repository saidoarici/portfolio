## Selected systems

### Payment Management

<img src="assets/payment-management.png" alt="Payment Management request workflow interface" width="100%" />

An enterprise workflow for payment requests, multi-stage approvals, documents, financial records, and ERP integrations. It brings request creation, invoice references, beneficiary data, auditability, and approval status into one controlled process.

**Focus:** finance automation · approval workflows · Odoo and QuickBooks integration · document handling · auditability

[Read the case study →](projects/payment-management.md)

### Anlık Bakiyem

<img src="assets/anlik-bakiyem.png" alt="Anlık Bakiyem ERP and accounting integrations interface" width="100%" />

A financial visibility and reconciliation platform that combines bank, Odoo, and QuickBooks data. It normalizes balances and transactions, detects differences, supports account matching, and gives finance teams a current operational view.

**Focus:** bank connectivity · ERP reconciliation · account matching · reporting · read-only AI connectors

[Read the case study →](projects/anlik-bakiyem.md)

### Rawabet

<img src="assets/rawabet-dashboard.png" alt="Rawabet sandbox operations dashboard" width="100%" />

An end-to-end logistics platform for orders, quotes, transportation, delivery, payments, invoices, drivers, vehicles, routes, and operational reporting. The system is designed for multilingual workflows and role-based administration.

**Core stack:** FastAPI · React · MongoDB · Docker · Mapbox · REST APIs

[Read the case study →](projects/rawabet.md)

### Event-driven WhatsApp Bot

<img src="assets/whatsapp-bot.png" alt="Rawabet event-driven WhatsApp notification flow builder" width="100%" />

A configurable notification engine connected to Rawabet. Operational events select bilingual templates, resolve user or admin recipients, and trigger the appropriate WhatsApp flow across the order and delivery lifecycle.

**Focus:** event-driven automation · English/Arabic templates · recipient routing · operational notifications · lifecycle coverage

[Read the case study →](projects/whatsapp-bot.md)
